## Chapter 12 Cheat Sheet: Full AAIA Exam Simulation & Time Management

### Exam Structure Snapshot
| Element | Value |
|---|---|
| Question count | ~90 scenario-based questions |
| Domains tested | AI Operations · AI Governance and Risk · AI Auditing Tools and Techniques |
| Score scale | 200–800 |
| Approx. passing score | ~450 (verify current value on ISACA's official site) |
| Question style | Scenario-heavy; definitions rarely stand alone |

### Question-Type Distribution to Expect
| Type | Approx. Weight | Strategy |
|---|---|---|
| Scenario-based | 40% | Identify the ONE best action, not just a correct one |
| Service/framework selection | 30% | Match option TYPE to what's asked (standard vs regulation vs framework) |
| Configuration | 20% | Know direction of thresholds — too low vs too high effects |
| Conceptual/comparison | 10% | Memorize crisp one-line differentiators |

### Pacing Strategy
| Checkpoint | Target Progress |
|---|---|
| 25% of time elapsed | ~25% of questions done |
| 50% of time elapsed | ~50% of questions done |
| 75% of time elapsed | ~75% of questions done |
| Final 10–15% of time | Dedicated flagged-question review pass |

**Rule:** If behind pace at any checkpoint, switch immediately to flag-and-move — never spend more than ~2x average time on one question during first pass.

### Elimination Strategy for Distractors
- Cut options with absolutes: "always," "never," "immediately disable," "reject outright."
- Cut options that answer a *different question type* (e.g., a regulation offered where a certifiable standard is asked).
- Cut options that treat a symptom instead of investigating root cause (common in AI Operations drift scenarios).
- Prefer options showing risk-based, proportional, documented responses over extreme actions.

### Framework Quick Comparison
| Framework | Nature | Certifiable? |
|---|---|---|
| NIST AI RMF | Voluntary risk guidance | No |
| ISO/IEC 42001 | AI management system standard | Yes |
| EU AI Act | Binding regulation | N/A (legal compliance, not certification) |
| COBIT | General IT governance | No (not AI-specific) |

### Explainability Quick Comparison
| Technique | Scope | Best For |
|---|---|---|
| SHAP | Global + local, theoretically consistent | Explaining individual decisions with rigor |
| LIME | Local surrogate approximation | Fast, approximate local explanation |
| Feature importance (global) | Aggregate | Overall model behavior, not individual cases |
| Partial dependence plots | Average marginal effect | Trend across a feature, not a single case |

### Final Review Pass Rule
Only change a flagged answer if you misread the scenario or question — not due to anxiety-driven second-guessing.

### Post-Exam Next Steps
- Note the scaled score band shown on your results (do not assume raw % = scaled score).
- If passed: begin continuing education tracking per ISACA's current CPE requirements (confirm hours/cycle on ISACA's site).
- If not passed: use the domain-level performance breakdown to target weak domains before retesting.
