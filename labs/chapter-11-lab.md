# Chapter 11 Lab: Bias, Fairness, Explainability Testing, and Third-Party/Model Provenance Assurance

**Estimated time:** 30 minutes

## Exercise

Review a sample SHAP output and disparate-impact-ratio calculation for a lending model, and document an audit finding with severity rating and recommended remediation

## Key takeaway

Fairness and provenance claims, whether internal or vendor-supplied, require independent audit validation rather than acceptance at face value.
