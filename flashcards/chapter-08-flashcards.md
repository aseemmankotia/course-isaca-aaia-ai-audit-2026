## Chapter 8: Regulatory Landscape: EU AI Act, NIST AI RMF, ISO/IEC 42001 — Flashcards

| # | Front (Question) | Back (Answer) |
|---|-----------------|---------------|
| 1 | EU AI Act: date prohibited practices (Article 5) became enforceable? | February 2, 2025. Exam tip: this is the earliest enforcement milestone — precedes GPAI obligations and high-risk obligations. |
| 2 | EU AI Act: date GPAI (general-purpose AI model) obligations apply? | August 2, 2025. Applies to providers of general-purpose AI models, including systemic-risk models with added obligations. |
| 3 | EU AI Act: date most Annex III high-risk system obligations apply? | August 2, 2026. Annex I products (regulated under other EU product laws, e.g., medical devices, machinery) get until August 2, 2027. |
| 4 | EU AI Act: max fine for Article 5 prohibited practices? | €35 million or 7% of global annual turnover, whichever is higher — the highest penalty tier. |
| 5 | EU AI Act: max fine for high-risk obligation violations (non-prohibited)? | €15 million or 3% of global annual turnover, whichever is higher. |
| 6 | EU AI Act four risk tiers? | Unacceptable (banned), High-risk (conformity assessment + documentation), Limited (transparency only, e.g. chatbots/deepfakes), Minimal (no legal obligation, voluntary codes encouraged). |
| 7 | NIST AI RMF four core functions? | Govern, Map, Measure, Manage. Govern is the cross-cutting foundation infused throughout the other three. |
| 8 | NIST AI RMF vs ISO/IEC 42001 — key difference? | NIST AI RMF: voluntary US guidance framework, not certifiable. ISO/IEC 42001: international, certifiable AI Management System standard (third-party audit possible), follows Annex SL structure. |
| 9 | ISO/IEC 42001 publication year and scope? | Published December 2023. First international standard specifying requirements for an AI Management System (AIMS), covering full AI lifecycle governance. |
| 10 | EU AI Act high-risk system core obligations (name 4)? | Risk management system, data governance, technical documentation + logging, human oversight — plus conformity assessment and EU database registration before market placement. |
| 11 | EU AI Act conformity assessment routes for high-risk Annex III systems? | Default: internal control/self-assessment (Annex VI) if harmonized standards applied. Escalates to third-party notified body assessment (Annex VII) for certain high-stakes uses (e.g., remote biometric ID) or when standards weren't applied. |
| 12 | Crosswalk approach — definition and exam use? | Mapping a single internal control set to the specific clauses of multiple frameworks (EU AI Act, NIST AI RMF, ISO 42001) so one evidence set satisfies overlapping requirements, avoiding duplicated compliance work. |
